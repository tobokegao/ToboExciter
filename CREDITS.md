## The pictures of the panel

The pedal, its leads and the ground on the panel are renders of a 3D model made for this plug-in with Blender (https://www.blender.org/). The reflections come from the studio lighting image that ships with Blender.

## The model

The signal processing was written for this plug-in. It was fitted to measurements of a hardware unit (sine sweeps at several levels and knob positions) and compared with a guitar re-amped through the same unit.
